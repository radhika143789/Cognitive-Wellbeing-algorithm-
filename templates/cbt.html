Behavioural Activation Planner

Depression often traps us in avoidance. BA breaks the cycle: plan a small activity, rate your mood before and after to build evidence that action helps.

💡 Behavioural Activation Tips

  • Start small — even 5 minutes counts.
  • Choose activities that used to bring you joy, or try something new.
  • Don't wait to "feel like it" — act first, motivation follows.
  • Track your mood before and after to build personal evidence.
  • Celebrate completing the activity, regardless of how you felt.
